I build things that sit at the intersection of machine learning, cybersecurity, and real hardware. My dissertation is an end-to-end AI vulnerability scanner — crawling, detecting, classifying risk with ML, explaining it with SHAP/LIME, then validating findings through automated pentesting. I care about systems that are correct, explainable, and not just impressive demos.

Currently: finishing my CS degree and looking for roles in security engineering or AI.

---

## Projects

### [GlitchForge](https://github.com/BelalIoT21/GlitchForge) &nbsp;·&nbsp; CS Dissertation
> AI-powered web vulnerability scanner — detection, ML risk scoring, XAI, automated pentesting

Full pipeline: crawl → detect (SQLi, XSS, CSRF) → ML risk classification → SHAP/LIME explainability → automated pentesting validation → prioritized report with PDF export.

`Python` `Flask` `scikit-learn` `React` `TypeScript` `SHAP` `LIME`

---

### [DecentraVote](https://github.com/BelalIoT21/DecentraVote) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Blockchain
> Tamper-proof, end-to-end encrypted decentralized voting on Ethereum

No central authority. Real-time on-chain results. Cryptographic vote integrity enforced at the smart contract level.

`Solidity` `ethers.js` `React` `Node.js`
